🎲 D20 Skill Roller

Let the dice decide whether a skill attempt works. Pick the skill, add the conditions that make it harder or easier, and roll. Meet or beat the target number to succeed. A natural 1 always fails with a complication, and a natural 20 always succeeds. Every roll is logged for the debrief.
